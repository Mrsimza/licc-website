📌 About the China Track

Through partnerships with Chinese companies and institutions, we aim to acquire skills and technology in:

  • Plastic recycling and brick-making for low‑cost housing
  • Steel fabrication and basic manufacturing
  • Machine operation and maintenance
  • Product design and quality control

We seek technology transfer, equipment supply, and training exchanges that will enable our community to produce its own building materials and products.
