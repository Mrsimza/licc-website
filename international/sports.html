📌 About the Sports Exchange

We seek to partner with countries renowned for excellence in football, athletics, and netball. Through exchanges, our coaches and youth can benefit from:

  • Advanced coaching techniques and training methodologies
  • Exposure to international competition
  • Sports science and player development practices
  • Cultural exchange and community building

Target countries: Brazil, Portugal (football); Kenya, Ethiopia (athletics); Australia, New Zealand (netball); Netherlands (sports science).
