📌 About the Arts & Culture Exchange

We aim to connect with countries that have rich artistic and cultural traditions. Potential exchange activities include:

  • Artist-in-residence programmes (inbound and outbound)
  • Workshops in traditional crafts, music, dance, and digital arts
  • Collaborative exhibitions and performances
  • Storytelling and cultural documentation projects

Target countries: Senegal (music, visual arts), Brazil (Afro-Brazilian arts), Italy (fine arts), India (traditional crafts), Ghana (cultural festivals).
