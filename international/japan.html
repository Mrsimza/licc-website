📌 About the Japan Track

We aim to collaborate with Japanese animation studios, creative institutions, and cultural organisations to:

  • Learn animation, storytelling, and content creation techniques
  • Develop South African stories into animated productions
  • Access software, production pipelines, and mentorship
  • Build a local animation industry that retains African IP

Our goal is to create authentic South African content for local and global audiences.
